How you get a desk

Post about @TheMutualFun on X, or quote one of their posts. Replies don't count. Your desk shows up within a minute or two. Your department is drawn from your handle and never changes, the way a Seat is dealt a fund.

The performance review

  • +10 for each post, up to 3 a day
  • +5 for each day you posted
  • +1 per like and reply, +2 per repost and quote, up to 50 a post
  • Likes are re-counted once a day.
  • On a tied score, whoever joined first sits higher.
